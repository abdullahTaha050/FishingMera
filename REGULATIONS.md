# Regulation data

The bundled version is `TR-6-2-2024-21-AMENDED-2025-12`, checked on 23 August 2026 and effective from 1 September 2024 through 31 August 2028. The base source is the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry's 6/2 Communiqué (2024/21); the 2025/12 amendment is tracked separately.

The mobile app contains a conservative summary for the target marine species plus the general 5 kg total-retention, rod/hook and marked-swimming-area rules. “Çinekop” and “sarıkanat” are presented under the 20 cm lüfer minimum because these names refer to growth stages, not separate legal rows. No special minimum is shown for zargana because the official table does not provide one.

This dataset is a dated convenience cache, not legal advice. Temporary local closures, harbour/security restrictions, protected areas and on-site signs override it. Before every release, compare the base communiqué and all amendments, update `checkedAt`, run the database tests, and show staleness in the client.
